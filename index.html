USE maven_fuzzy_factory;

-- This creates a reusable master data model that Power BI can read instantly
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW vw_portfolio_ecommerce_performance AS
SELECT 
    DATE_FORMAT(ws.created_at, '%Y-%m-01') AS performance_month,
    COALESCE(ws.utm_source, 'Direct / Organic') AS traffic_channel,
    COALESCE(ws.utm_campaign, 'Non-Paid Brand') AS campaign_name,
    
    -- Volumetric Counts (Answers Q1 & Q3)
    COUNT(DISTINCT ws.website_session_id) AS total_sessions,
    COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) AS total_orders,
    
    -- Financial Performance Metrics (Answers Q4)
    SUM(o.price_usd) AS gross_revenue,
    ROUND(AVG(o.price_usd), 2) AS revenue_per_order
FROM website_sessions ws
LEFT JOIN orders o 
    ON ws.website_session_id = o.website_session_id
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3
ORDER BY 1 ASC;
SELECT * FROM vw_portfolio_ecommerce_performance;

The goal: study admission trends, length of stay, and the relationship between visit costs and insurance coverage, using 2011–2022 data. The Maven Analytics dataset is synthetic data on about 1,000 patients, so it does not describe real people.

What the dashboard shows

  • 27,891 encounters, with an average duration of 7.27 hours across all visit types.
  • Annual encounters peaked at about 3,900, with a second peak of about 3,500. The 2022 data appears to cover only part of the year.
  • Inpatient visits had the highest average total cost. Urgent care had the highest average out-of-pocket cost.
